Shelf Style Reference

Fonts, colors and text rules for shelf.madluna.ca

Fonts

Two typefaces, both loaded from Google Fonts. Same pair as the main madluna site.

Body Font — Plus Jakarta Sans

the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog

used for all body copy, paragraphs, labels, buttons, form fields, nav links and metadata.

300 light 400 regular 500 medium 600 semibold 700 bold
font-family: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif;
--font-sans

Header Font — Playfair Display

the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog

used for headings (h1–h6), the shelf. logo, section titles, and album/item titles in cover-art fallbacks. Also used italic for accent phrases in hero copy.

400 regular 600 semibold 700 bold 700 bold italic
font-family: 'Playfair Display', serif;
--font-serif

Import

https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Playfair+Display:ital,wght@0,400..900;1,400..900&family=Plus+Jakarta+Sans:ital,wght@0,200..800;1,200..800&display=swap

Text Casing

site text is strictly lowercase by default.

anywhere the word "shelf" appears, it always ends with a period and stays lowercase — "shelf."
