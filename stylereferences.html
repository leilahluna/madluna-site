Color Reference

Complete color palette used throughout the Madluna site.

Fonts

Two typefaces, both loaded from Google Fonts in styles.css. Same pair as shelf.

Body Font — Plus Jakarta Sans

the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog

set on body — used for all body copy, taglines, card text, nav links, buttons and status badges.

300 light 400 regular 500 medium 600 semibold 700 bold
font-family: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif;

Header Font — Playfair Display

the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog

used for the madluna logo (600, 2px letter-spacing), hero h1 (700, 3px letter-spacing) and section titles (1.8rem).

400 regular 700 bold 700 bold italic
font-family: 'Playfair Display', serif;

Import

@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Playfair+Display:wght@400;700&family=Plus+Jakarta+Sans:wght@300;400;500;600;700&display=swap');

Text Casing

site text is strictly lowercase by default.

exception: proper nouns and brand/product names keep their real capitalization — e.g. Plex, Overseerr, Minecraft.
